## Get challenges that fit your skills.

Tell us what you like building. We’ll email you relevant challenges with the task, prizes and rules, so you can decide whether to enter.

Send your preferred topics and, if you have one, a project or demo you worked on. Include “Yes, send me relevant challenge updates” if you want to hear from us. We only add you after receiving your permission. You can stop by replying “Stop”.

## What the technical pages are for

The brief is the friendly starting point, not a replacement for the rules. Each challenge page contains the full input format, scoring weights, limits, sample questions, model policy, and what happens to your submission. Read those details before you submit.

You can use an AI assistant, a starter repo, or a collaborator. You still own the work and are responsible for checking that it runs, follows the rules, and does not include private keys.
